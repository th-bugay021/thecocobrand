How to Find Your Perfect Lip Shade for Any Skin Tone

📅 April 2025 ⏱ 5 min read

Finding the right lip colour can feel overwhelming — especially when you are surrounded by hundreds of shades promising to be universally flattering. The truth is, the perfect shade is not one-size-fits-all. It starts with understanding your undertone, your skin depth, and the mood you want to express.

First, Know Your Undertone

Your undertone is the subtle hue beneath your skin surface — and it is the single most important factor in choosing a lip colour. There are three main undertones:

  • Warm undertones — your skin has golden, peachy, or yellow hues. Look for shades with orange, coral, or warm brown bases.
  • Cool undertones — your skin leans pink, red, or bluish. Berry, wine, and blue-based reds will look stunning on you.
  • Neutral undertones — a mix of warm and cool. Most shades will work beautifully.
Quick test: look at the veins on your wrist. Blue or purple veins = cool undertone. Green veins = warm. A mix of both = neutral.

Match Depth to Skin Depth

Beyond undertone, the depth of your skin matters. Deeper skin tones can carry bold, saturated shades with ease — think rich plums, deep berries, and vibrant reds. Lighter skin tones often shine in softer versions of these same families.

At The Coco Brand, every shade was tested across a wide range of skin tones before it was released. Scarlet Kiss was specifically designed to pop on deeper melanin-rich skin without looking muddy or flat.

The Occasion Rule

Your lip colour should match the energy of your day. A bold red or deep plum is powerful for evenings and events. A warm nude or glossy pink feels effortless for daytime and casual outings.

  • Day out with friends → Bare Velvet or Crystal Gloss
  • Work or meetings → Cocoa Dream or Bare Velvet
  • Date night → Scarlet Kiss or Midnight Plum
  • Events and celebrations → Coco Rose or Midnight Plum
Never judge a shade on the bullet or in the tube. Always swatch on your inner wrist or lower lip before deciding — the way a colour looks on your skin is everything.

When In Doubt, Go Nude

A well-chosen nude is the most powerful shade in your collection. The key is finding one slightly deeper than your natural lip colour — too light and it washes you out, too dark and it becomes the main event.

Bare Velvet was made specifically with deeper skin tones in mind. Most mainstream nudes are formulated for light skin and look chalky on melanin-rich lips. Bare Velvet pulls warm and rich — the way a true nude should feel.
