Why Your Lips Need a Skincare Routine Too

📅 January 2025 ⏱ 3 min read

We spend time and money caring for the skin on our face — cleansing, toning, moisturising, SPF. But the lips are often an afterthought. Your lips are some of the most delicate skin on your body, and they need consistent care to stay healthy, smooth, and ready for colour.

Why Lips Age Faster

The skin on your lips is thinner than the rest of your face and contains no oil glands — which means it cannot moisturise itself. It relies entirely on the products you apply and the water you drink. Without regular care, lips become dry, chapped, and lined.

The Daily Lip Routine

Morning and night, your lips deserve attention. Here is what a simple but effective lip care routine looks like:

  • Morning: Apply a nourishing lip balm with SPF. Sun exposure is one of the leading causes of lip darkening and premature ageing.
  • Before colour: Let your balm absorb for two minutes, then apply your lip colour. This prevents the formula from drying out your lips.
  • Night: Apply a thick layer of overnight lip mask or a rich balm like shea butter or castor oil. Let it work while you sleep.
Sleeping with a humidifier in your room makes a noticeable difference to lip hydration — especially in dry seasons or air-conditioned environments.

The Exfoliation Step

Two to three times a week, gently exfoliate your lips to remove dead skin. You can use a dedicated lip scrub, a soft toothbrush, or even a small amount of sugar mixed with honey. Gentle circular motions for 30 seconds are all it takes.

Never pick at dry skin on your lips — this causes micro-tears that lead to darkening and scarring over time. Exfoliate instead, then follow immediately with moisture.

Hydration From Within

No topical product can fully compensate for poor internal hydration. Drink enough water daily — at least 8 glasses — and incorporate water-rich foods like cucumber, watermelon, and leafy greens into your diet.
