5 Ways to Make Your Lip Colour Last All Day

📅 March 2025 ⏱ 3 min read

We have all been there — you apply your lip colour perfectly in the morning and by midday it has faded, feathered, or smudged. The good news? Long-lasting lip colour is less about the product and more about the technique.

1. Start with Exfoliated Lips

Dead skin and dry patches are the enemy of long-wear colour. Before anything else, gently exfoliate your lips with a lip scrub or a soft toothbrush in small circular motions. Follow with a thin layer of lip balm — but let it absorb fully for at least two minutes before applying colour.

2. Line and Fill

Lip liner is not just for the edges. Once you have lined your lips with a matching pencil, fill in the entire lip. This creates a colour base that acts as an anchor — your lipstick has something to grip onto and is far less likely to fade over time.

Match your liner as closely as possible to your lipstick shade, or go one shade darker for added definition and extra staying power.

3. Apply in Thin Layers

One thick coat of lipstick looks heavy and fades fast. Instead, apply a thin first layer, blot with a tissue, then apply a second layer. This builds pigment in a lightweight way that moves with you instead of cracking or transferring.

4. The Powder Trick

This is the professional secret. After your first layer, hold a single-ply tissue over your lips and lightly dust translucent setting powder through it. The powder locks the colour without dulling the finish. Then apply your second coat for a fresh, lasting look.

5. Set and Protect

Avoid eating oily foods, and use a straw when drinking — especially in the first hour after application. Touch up with a small amount of product after meals rather than fully reapplying.

  • Avoid rubbing your lips together after applying — press them gently instead
  • Keep your lipstick in your bag for quick touch-ups
  • Matte formulas generally last longer than glossy ones
  • Drink from the back of the cup or use a straw
With Coco Brand formulas, you are already starting with a high-pigment, long-wear base. These techniques will take your wear time from good to all day.
