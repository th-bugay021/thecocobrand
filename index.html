Skincare

Your Lip Care Routine

Healthy lips start with the right routine. Here's how to get the most from your Coco colours.

01
Hydrate & Prep

Start with a hydrating lip balm to smooth and moisturise. Let it absorb for 2 minutes before applying colour for a flawless base.

✦ Morning Routine
02
Apply with Precision

Line your lips with a matching pencil, then fill in with your Coco lip colour. Start from the centre and blend outward for even coverage.

✦ Application Tip
03
Lock In the Colour

Press a thin tissue over your lips and lightly dust translucent powder over it. This locks in your colour for all-day wear.

✦ Long Wear Hack
